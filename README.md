# 🧠 EmoHealth Analytics
### Mood Swings & Body Health Monitoring Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

EmoHealth Analytics is an end-to-end healthcare and wellness analytics dashboard developed using **Power BI**. The project integrates emotional health indicators, physical health metrics, lifestyle activities, diet patterns, and wellness recommendations into a unified decision-support platform.

The dashboard helps monitor overall well-being, identify health risks, analyze behavioral patterns, and provide actionable recommendations through interactive visualizations and analytics.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Short sleep duration is associated with increased stress and negative mood patterns.
- Walking and yoga contribute significantly to mood improvement.
- Elevated sugar levels combined with high stress increase health risk.
- Better diet compliance and hydration support mood stabilization.
- Lifestyle improvements positively impact overall wellness scores.
